### 🏗️ 2. Product I'm Building: `Onboard.track`

> **ERP Client Onboarding Dashboard** — answers *"Is this client ready to go live?"* in under 10 seconds. No spreadsheet. No weekly call. Just data.

[![PRD](https://img.shields.io/badge/📄_Read_Full_PRD-v1.0-blue?style=flat-square)](03_PRD_Onboard_Track/PRD_Onboard_Track.md)
[![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Building_MVP-orange?style=flat-square)](#)
[![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Stack-Next.js_+_Supabase_+_Tailwind-black?style=flat-square)](#)

**The Problem:**
Every ERP implementation I've worked on runs on shared Google Sheets, email chains, and gut feel. Blockers are discovered on weekly calls — 5 days too late. Sign-offs get lost in inboxes. Nobody can answer "are we ready to go live?" with confidence.

**The Solution — 5 MVP Features:**

| # | Feature | What It Solves |
|---|---------|---------------|
| 1 | **Project Setup & Client Dashboard** | Single source of truth per client |
| 2 | **Module Progress Tracker** | 7-stage pipeline with real-time visibility |
| 3 | **Sign-off Workflow** | Replaces email sign-offs — client approves via one-click link |
| 4 | **Client Readiness Score (0–100%)** | Data-driven answer to "can we go live?" |
| 5 | **Team Daily View** | Personalized "what needs my attention today" |

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Projects tracked in the tool (replacing spreadsheets)
