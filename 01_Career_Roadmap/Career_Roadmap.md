## 🏗️ The 3–6 Month Project-Building Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–4) — *Learn + Document*

#### Project 1: Product Teardown Portfolio (3 detailed teardowns)
> **Goal:** Demonstrate product thinking without needing to build software

Pick 3 products you use daily and write deep teardowns:

| Teardown Element | What to Include |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Problem Statement** | What user pain does this product solve? |
| **User Personas** | Who are the primary, secondary users? |
| **Feature Audit** | Map core vs. nice-to-have features |
| **Competitive Analysis** | How does it compare to 2-3 alternatives? |
| **Metrics Hypothesis** | What KPIs likely drive their decisions? |
| **Improvement Proposal** | Your redesign with wireframes (use Figma/FigJam) |
| **Prioritization** | RICE-score your proposed improvements |

**Suggested products to teardown:**
1. A Campus ERP product (you have domain expertise from Turtil)
2. A FinTech app (connects to your MBA FinTech coursework)
3. A productivity/SaaS tool you use daily

**Deliverable:** 3 published Medium articles or Notion pages with your analysis

---

### Phase 2: Build (Weeks 5–12) — *Ship Real Products*

#### Project 2: Build a Micro-SaaS Product (End-to-End PM Execution)
> **Goal:** Show you can take a product from idea → shipped

You don't need to code it all yourself. Use no-code/low-code tools or collaborate with a developer.

**Recommended approach:**
1. **Identify a real problem** from your Turtil/PACS experience
   - Example: "Release readiness tracker for QA teams"
   - Example: "Client onboarding status dashboard for ERP implementations"
2. **Write a full PRD (Product Requirements Document)**
   - Problem statement, user stories, acceptance criteria
   - Wireframes (Figma), information architecture
   - Success metrics (KPIs you'll track post-launch)
3. **Build an MVP** using:
   - **No-code:** Bubble.io, Softr, Glide, or Retool
   - **Low-code:** If you want to use your CS skills — Next.js + Supabase
4. **Launch it** — Product Hunt, LinkedIn post, share with QA communities
5. **Track metrics** — even 10 users is valuable; document learnings

**Deliverable:** Live product + PRD + launch metrics document
